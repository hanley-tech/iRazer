⭐ Stop the Nommo V2 from turning off

The most common Nommo V2 complaint: it drops into standby and powers off during quiet moments, and there's no way to disable that on the device (the firmware's standby timer is read-only). iRazer's Keep Speakers Awake toggle keeps the audio path alive with a brief inaudible pulse once a minute, so the speakers never idle out. This is the reason iRazer was built.

Common Nommo V2 questions

Why does my Razer Nommo V2 keep turning off / going to sleep?

It auto-powers-down after audio silence, and the standby timer can't be changed on the device. Turn on Keep Speakers Awake in iRazer to stop it.

How do I change the Nommo V2 EQ on a Mac without Synapse?

Synapse is Windows-only. iRazer gives the Nommo V2 a 10-band EQ with presets in the Sound tab, over USB.

How do I control Nommo V2 volume on a Mac?

iRazer exposes the device's DSP master volume in the Sound tab, alongside macOS system volume.

Can I change the Nommo V2 RGB lighting on a Mac?

Brightness (and turning the lights off) works today. Full RGB color/effects are in progress — that control is on the Nommo V2's Bluetooth channel, which iRazer is decoding.

Does Razer Synapse work with the Nommo V2 on macOS?

No — Synapse is Windows-only. iRazer is the free, open-source native macOS alternative.
